Petrol Pump Solution for TallyPrime

The Petrol Pump Solution is a purpose-built Tally Prime add-on designed specifically for petrol pump businesses. It simplifies daily operations including fuel sales, stock tracking, and credit management — all within the Tally environment.

From cash, card, and credit sales to nozzle readings, tank stock monitoring, and vehicle-wise accounting, everything is managed seamlessly without the need for any external software.

Built around real petrol station workflows, the solution ensures smooth handling of shift-wise operations, daily closing, and reconciliation processes with minimal manual effort.

It helps petrol pump owners improve accuracy, reduce leakage, and maintain complete control over financial and inventory data — fully integrated with TallyPrime.

WHAT IT DOES FOR YOUR BUSINESS

Complete Fuel Station Management Inside Tally

Our Petrol Pump Solution brings all essential fuel station operations into one integrated system inside Tally — helping you manage sales, stock, credit, and compliance with ease.

  • All sale types in one place — cash, card (debit, credit, petro-card), and credit sales fully integrated with Tally accounts
  • Credit customer management with slip-wise and vehicle-wise tracking, plus auto-generated bills and statements
  • Nozzle & tank control with daily meter readings, dip stick monitoring, and accurate tank-wise stock tracking
  • Complete sales reporting including DSR, daily summary, item-wise and tank-wise reports
  • Multi-product support for petrol, diesel, oil, and other fuel-related items with separate tracking
  • Tax & compliance ready system with built-in reporting to ensure smooth regulatory adherence
