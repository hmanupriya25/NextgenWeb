What It Does

Complete fuel station management — all inside TallyPrime

The Petrol Pump Module brings every daily operation of your fuel station into one integrated system inside TallyPrime. From cash, card, and credit sales to nozzle readings, tank stock monitoring, driver cash management, and vehicle-wise accounting — everything is handled without any external software.

Built around real petrol station workflows — shift-wise operations, daily closing, credit slip management, and reconciliation — all with minimal manual effort and maximum accuracy.

Cash & Card SalesAll payment types posted directly into Tally
Vehicle-wise Credit SalesSlip-wise & vehicle-wise credit tracking with auto billing
Nozzle & Tank ManagementDaily meter readings, dip stick, tank-wise stock control
DSR & Shift ReportsDaily Sales Report & Shift Summary generated automatically
Key Benefits

Why petrol stations choose our module

Everything you gain when you switch from manual records or separate software to our fully integrated TallyPrime petrol pump solution.

All Sale Types in One System

Cash, card (debit, credit, petro-card), and credit sales — all handled in a single module, fully posting into TallyPrime accounts automatically.

Vehicle-wise Credit Management

Track credit sales slip-wise and vehicle-wise. Auto-generate bills or statements at periodic intervals with zero manual follow-up.

Nozzle & Tank Control

Record daily meter and nozzle readings, manage dip stick measurements, and maintain accurate tank-wise stock levels at all times.

Driver & Salesman Management

Track driver cash collections, manage salesman/filler activity, and maintain clear records of tanker sales with full accountability.

Complete Daily Reporting

DSR, Daily Shift Summary, item-wise and tank-wise reports generated automatically — giving management a complete daily picture.

Auto Rate Updates

Daily fuel price changes are updated automatically across the system — no manual re-entry, no billing errors on price change days.
