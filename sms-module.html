Tally SMS Module

Instant SMS Notifications Directly from TallyPrime

Keep your customers informed with real-time SMS notifications sent directly from TallyPrime. The Tally SMS Module automatically sends transaction alerts such as invoices, receipts, payment reminders, and promotional messages, helping businesses improve customer communication and strengthen relationships.

With seamless integration into TallyPrime, businesses can automate customer communication without manual effort. Important updates are delivered instantly, improving transparency, efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

Why Choose the Tally SMS Module?

Automate your customer communication with powerful SMS features integrated with TallyPrime.

Automatic Invoice SMS

Send instant SMS notifications to customers immediately after creating a sales invoice, including invoice number, amount, and payment details.

Receipt Confirmation

Automatically notify customers when a payment receipt is recorded, providing instant confirmation of successful payment.

Promotional SMS Campaigns

Send promotional offers, seasonal greetings, new product announcements, and marketing messages directly from Tally.

Customer Mobile Integration

Store customer mobile numbers within the Tally ledger master and use them automatically for SMS communication.

Bulk SMS Facility

Send SMS messages to multiple customers simultaneously for announcements, marketing campaigns, and business updates.

Payment Reminder Alerts

Send timely payment reminders to customers with outstanding balances, helping improve collections and reduce overdue payments.

Customisable SMS Templates

Create personalised SMS templates for different transaction types with your company branding and communication style.

Real-Time Notifications

Customers receive important transaction updates instantly, improving transparency and customer satisfaction.

Easy to Use

The module integrates seamlessly with TallyPrime, requiring no additional software or complex processes.

Secure & Reliable

SMS messages are delivered through trusted SMS gateways ensuring fast, reliable, and secure communication.

How Tally SMS Module Works

Automate customer communication with a simple and efficient SMS workflow.

1. Transaction Created

Create invoices, receipts, or transactions in TallyPrime as usual.

2. SMS Module Processing

The SMS Module automatically captures transaction details from TallyPrime.

3. SMS Sent

The message is processed through a secure SMS gateway.

4. Customer Receives

Customers receive instant notifications on their mobile devices.
