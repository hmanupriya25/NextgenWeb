SMS Features

Everything the SMS Module does for you

Automate customer communication with powerful SMS features built directly into TallyPrime.

Automatic Invoice SMS

Send instant SMS to customers the moment a sales invoice is created in TallyPrime — including invoice number, amount, and due date.

Receipt Confirmation

Automatically notify customers when a payment receipt is recorded — providing instant confirmation of successful payment.

Payment Reminder Alerts

Send timely payment reminders to customers with outstanding balances — improving collections and reducing overdue payments.

Promotional SMS Campaigns

Send promotional offers, seasonal greetings, new product announcements, and marketing messages directly from TallyPrime.

Bulk SMS Facility

Send SMS to multiple customers simultaneously for announcements, marketing campaigns, and business updates in one action.

Customisable SMS Templates

Create personalised SMS templates for each transaction type with your company branding and preferred communication style.

Customer Mobile Integration

Customer mobile numbers stored in the Tally ledger master are used automatically — no separate contact management needed.

Real-Time Notifications

Customers receive important transaction updates the moment they happen — improving transparency and trust.

Secure & Reliable Delivery

Messages are sent through trusted SMS gateways ensuring fast, reliable, and secure delivery to your customers.

How It Works

Automated in 4 simple steps

Once configured, the SMS Module runs fully automatically — no extra steps for your team.

Step 1

Transaction Created

Create invoices, receipts, or any voucher in TallyPrime as you normally would

Step 2

Module Processes

The SMS Module captures the transaction details and builds the message from your template

Step 3

Sent via Gateway

Message is routed through a secure, trusted SMS gateway for reliable delivery

Step 4

Customer Receives

Customer gets an instant notification on their mobile — no app or internet needed

WhatsApp Features

Powerful WhatsApp communication built into TallyPrime

Send faster, communicate smarter — the WhatsApp module works alongside TallyPrime to automate business communication.

Instant Auto-Send on Save

Press Ctrl+A to save a voucher — the WhatsApp goes out to the customer before you open the next entry. Zero extra keystrokes.

PDF Invoice as Attachment

Invoices are sent as proper PDF documents inside the WhatsApp chat — not just links. Customers can open and save them instantly.

Bulk Sharing to Multiple Parties

Share outstanding reminders, ledger reports, and invoices to multiple customers at once — from Statement of Account or Sales Register.

Payment Link & QR Code

Include UPI payment links or QR codes inside the WhatsApp invoice — making it easier for customers to pay immediately.

Delivery Reports

Track message delivery status inside TallyPrime — see which messages were delivered and read, per customer, per invoice.

Multi-Account Reports

Share All Ledger Accounts, Confirmation of Accounts, and other multi-account reports to multiple parties in one action.

Digital Signature on Documents

Include your digital signature on invoices and reports shared via WhatsApp — adding authenticity and professionalism.

Multiple File Formats

Share documents as PDF, JPEG, or Excel based on your customer's preference and the type of report being shared.

Official WhatsApp Business API

Powered by the official Meta-approved WhatsApp Business API — no QR scanning, no phone dependency, no risk of bans.

How It Works

WhatsApp delivered in seconds — automatically

From voucher save to customer's WhatsApp — the entire process happens automatically with no manual steps.

Step 1

Save Voucher in Tally

Create and save any voucher or open any report in TallyPrime

Step 2

Module Processes

WhatsApp module converts the document to PDF and applies the approved message template

Step 3

Sent via Business API

Message is dispatched through the official WhatsApp Business API — server-side, no phone needed

Step 4

Delivered & Reported

Customer receives the PDF on WhatsApp. Delivery status is logged back inside TallyPrime
