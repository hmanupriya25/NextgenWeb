Common questions

What is TDL?

TDL stands for Tally Definition Language. It is the language used to extend TallyPrime with custom screens, reports, print formats, fields and workflows.

How much does a customization cost?

It depends on the scope. A report or print-format change is far smaller than a full module. After we review your requirement we give you a clear quote.

Will it affect my existing Tally data?

We build customizations on top of your existing Tally setup and test them on a copy of your data first, so your live company is not put at risk.

Will it keep working when Tally updates?

A well-built customization keeps working through normal updates. For a major TallyPrime release we recommend testing first, and we can update the customization if needed.

Can you connect Tally to our other software?

Yes. We connect TallyPrime with ERP, CRM, POS, payroll, attendance and industry applications, so data moves between systems without re-typing.
