Professional Tally Customization Services

Tally Customization (TDL) enables businesses to extend and personalize Tally’s capabilities to meet their specific operational requirements. From customizing the user interface and designing tailored reports to modifying print formats, automating processes, and developing industry-specific functionalities, TDL helps businesses maximize productivity and efficiency.

At NextGen SoftTech Pvt Ltd, an authorized Tally Partner with over 20 years of industry experience, we specialize in delivering innovative and reliable Tally customization solutions. Our team of experienced Tally consultants and developers has successfully implemented hundreds of customization projects across diverse industries, including Manufacturing, Distribution, Trading, Retail, and Services.

Our customization services include custom invoice and voucher formats, specialized MIS reports, additional data fields, workflow automation, approval systems, user-specific controls, and industry-focused modules designed to streamline business operations.

Whether you need a simple report modification or a comprehensive business solution integrated within Tally, we provide customized, scalable, and cost-effective solutions that align with your business goals while ensuring seamless integration with your existing Tally environment.

KEY FEATURES & BENEFITS

Customize TallyPrime to Fit Your Business

Transform TallyPrime into a solution tailored specifically to your business processes, workflows, and reporting requirements.

  • Custom print formats with specific columns, notes, and page sizes
  • Professionally designed invoices, purchase orders, and business documents
  • Additional fields to capture industry-specific information
  • Advanced user access controls and security rules
  • Custom data synchronization and transformation processes
EXTEND TALLYPRIME FUNCTIONALITY

Go Beyond Standard Tally Features

Add powerful new capabilities and automate business operations without changing your existing Tally environment.

  • Develop completely new business functions and workflows
  • Create custom modules tailored to your industry requirements
  • Build extensions directly inside TallyPrime
  • Integrate external applications seamlessly
  • Enable web access, mobile access, SMS alerts, and notifications
SYSTEM INTEGRATION

Connect TallyPrime with Your Business Ecosystem

Integrate TallyPrime with other business applications to eliminate duplicate data entry and improve operational efficiency.

  • Integrate employee attendance and payroll systems
  • Connect third-party ERP, CRM, POS, and industry applications
  • Automate data exchange between multiple systems
  • Reduce manual work and improve data accuracy
  • Leverage TallyPrime's accounting, financial, and statutory capabilities
