Frequently asked questions

What threats does Bitdefender protect against?

Bitdefender protects against viruses, ransomware, phishing, zero-day exploits, rootkits, trojans, spyware, adware, and malicious websites — covering every major category of cyber threat.

Which devices and operating systems are supported?

Bitdefender Total Security covers Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS — all from a single subscription. Antivirus Plus and Internet Security are designed primarily for Windows PCs.

Will it slow down my computer?

No. Bitdefender is consistently rated as having minimal impact on system performance. It runs scans intelligently in the background so your speed and experience are never compromised.

Yes. Bitdefender's powerful scan and removal engine detects and eliminates existing viruses and malware — restoring your device to a clean, safe state.
