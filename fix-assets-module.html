Fixed Asset Management

A fully integrated Fixed Assets module for Tally ERP 9, giving your business complete control, visibility, and compliance over every asset you own.

Say goodbye to Excel-based asset spreadsheets.

The Fixed Assets Module for Tally ERP 9 replaces manual, error-prone spreadsheets with a fully integrated system that captures every asset-related transaction directly in your Tally database. It generates all required reports — statutory, audit, and management — on a real-time basis.

Developed as a Tally ERP 9 add-on, it works seamlessly alongside Tally's inventory, accounting, and payroll modules — extending your existing ERP without disrupting your workflow.

Multi-companyMulti-locationMulti-branch

What it does for your business

Complete asset visibility

Know the exact count, location, and condition of every asset across all your branches and departments — at any point in time.

Statutory compliance built in

Generates all required reports as per Income Tax Act and Companies Act 2013 — ready for auditors without any extra effort.

Depreciation on autopilot

Monthly and yearly depreciation is calculated and posted automatically — no manual entries, no errors.

Prevent asset misuse

Track each asset individually with a Unique ID and barcode. Run physical verification to reconcile assets against records.

GST-ready transactions

Handle input and output tax on asset purchases and sales. Supports car, mobile, and below-1-year asset entries with GST.

Rental & lease tracking

Manage rental and lease assets with complete tracking — rental returns, pending assets, and audit trails all in one place.
