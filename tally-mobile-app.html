Biz Analyst gives you instant, secure access to your Tally data, any time and anywhere. At your desk, in a meeting or on the move, live dashboards, reports and graphs help you decide faster, without waiting to get back to the office.

It is built for business owners, managers, accountants and sales teams who rely on Tally data every day, and it works on both Android and iPhone.

How it works

Getting started is simple. Just three steps.

  1. Install the app

    Download Biz Analyst on Android or iPhone and connect it securely to your Tally.

  2. Automatic sync

    Your Tally data, including sales, stock, reports and vouchers, syncs automatically.

  3. Manage anywhere

    Open reports, send invoices, track payments and run your business from anywhere.

Common questions

Which phones does Biz Analyst work on?

It works on both Android phones and iPhones.

Which versions of Tally does it connect to?

Biz Analyst connects to your Tally data, including TallyPrime and Tally.ERP 9. We check your version when we set it up.

Can I control who sees my data?

Yes. You can invite new users and manage access for existing users, and sales team members get limited access.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. There is a free 7-day trial. Ask us and we will help you start it on your own Tally data.

Who sets it up and supports it?

We do. As authorized Tally partners in Sri Lanka, we configure Biz Analyst for your business and support you afterwards.
