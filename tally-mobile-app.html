Common questions

Does it replace Tally?

No. Tally stays your accounting system. Biz Analyst reads from it and sends entries back to it.

Which Tally versions work?

Biz Analyst syncs with TallyPrime and Tally ERP 9. We will confirm your version during the demo.

Can staff only see what they need?

Yes. You control which reports and features each team member can open.

Do I need an iPhone or an Android phone?

Either one. The app is available for both.

Who helps if something goes wrong?

NextGen sets it up and supports it. Call us on +94 11 4 616 055 or email sales@ngst.lk.
