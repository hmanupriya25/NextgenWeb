Systems We Integrate With TallyPrime

Connect your existing business applications with TallyPrime for automated and accurate data flow.

Point of Sale (POS) Systems

Automatically transfer sales invoices, customer information, payments, and inventory updates from POS systems into TallyPrime.

Hospital Management Systems

Integrate patient billing, pharmacy sales, laboratory charges, doctor consultation fees, and payment transactions directly with TallyPrime.

Weighbridge Management Systems

Automatically record vehicle weights, material movements, and weighbridge transactions into TallyPrime for accurate inventory and accounting.

E-Commerce Platforms

Synchronise online orders, customer details, inventory information, and payment transactions with TallyPrime.

Manufacturing Systems

Integrate production data, material consumption, stock movements, and finished goods transactions with TallyPrime.

CRM Systems

Synchronise customer records, sales orders, payment information, and receivables between CRM applications and TallyPrime.

Custom Business Applications

Integrate virtually any Windows, web-based, or cloud application with TallyPrime using APIs, databases, web services, or custom integration methods.

Our Tally Integration Process

A structured approach to deliver reliable, secure, and scalable integration solutions.

01. Requirement Analysis

We understand your business processes, existing software environment, and integration objectives to identify the best solution approach.

02. Solution Design

Our team designs an efficient integration architecture and defines the data flow between TallyPrime and connected systems.

03. Development

We develop customised integration components, APIs, middleware, and automation workflows based on your requirements.

04. Testing

05. Deployment
