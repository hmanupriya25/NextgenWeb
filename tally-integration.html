What We Do

Seamlessly connect TallyPrime with your business applications

Modern businesses run on multiple software systems — POS at the counter, HMS in the hospital, e-commerce online, CRM for sales. But when these systems don't talk to each other, your team wastes hours manually re-entering the same data into TallyPrime.

Our Tally Integration Solutions eliminate that gap. We build secure, reliable connections between TallyPrime and any third-party system — so data flows automatically, accurately, and in real time.

Without IntegrationStaff manually copy data between systems every day — slow, error-prone, expensive.
With NextGen IntegrationData flows automatically from every system into TallyPrime — accurate, instant, effortless.
Systems We Integrate

Connect TallyPrime with any system

From retail POS to hospital management, weighbridges to e-commerce — we have built integrations across every major industry and application type.

Point of Sale (POS)

Automatically push sales invoices, customer data, payments, and inventory updates from any POS system into TallyPrime.

Hospital Management (HMS)

CRM Systems

ERP Systems

Bridge your existing ERP with TallyPrime for unified financial data, accounts, and reporting across the entire organization.
