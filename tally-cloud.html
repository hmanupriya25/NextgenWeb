What is Tally on Cloud?

Your Tally data, hosted securely on the cloud.

Tally on Cloud means your Tally software and company data are hosted on a dedicated remote server managed by us. Instead of running Tally on a local PC or office server, your team connects securely over the internet.

Experience the complete Tally experience without being tied to a single machine or location. It is built for modern businesses that require flexibility without compromising on security or speed.

Key Benefits

Why businesses choose Tally on Cloud

Experience the freedom of cloud-hosted accounting without the IT headaches.

Always On, Always Accessible

Access your Tally data 24/7 from your office, home, or while traveling — on any Windows PC, laptop or Mac.

No Server Maintenance

We handle server setup, updates, backups, and monitoring so your IT team doesn’t need to manage any infrastructure.

Multi-User, Multi-Location

Multiple users across different branches can work on the same Tally company simultaneously in real time.

Data Security & Daily Backups

Enterprise-grade servers with daily automated backups, firewall protection, and restricted access controls.

Disaster Recovery Ready

Hardware failure, power cuts, or local disasters won’t affect your data. Everything is safe on the cloud.

Scalable as You Grow

Add users or storage anytime without buying new hardware. All deployments use genuine licensed Tally software.

Who is this for?

Ideal for growing businesses that need flexibility

Tally on Cloud is designed for businesses that require secure, reliable, and remote access to their accounting data.

Multiple Branches & Remote Teams

Reduced IT Overhead

Startups & SMEs
