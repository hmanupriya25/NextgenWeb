Key Benefits

Complete control over every asset you own

Our Fixed Assets Module gives you end-to-end visibility and automation — from purchase to disposal — directly inside TallyPrime.

Complete Asset Visibility

Know the exact count, location, and condition of every asset across all branches and departments — in real time, without leaving Tally.

Depreciation on Autopilot

Automatic monthly and yearly depreciation posting with zero manual entry. Supports IT Act and Companies Act rates — always accurate.

Prevent Asset Misuse

Track every asset with a Unique ID and barcode. Run physical verifications and reconcile against records to catch discrepancies instantly.

Statutory Compliance Built In

Generate all required reports — FAR, depreciation charts, transfer reports — as per Income Tax Act and Companies Act 2013 standards.

Rental & Lease Tracking

Manage rental and lease assets with complete tracking — rental returns, pending assets, and full audit history all in one integrated system.

Multi-Company & Multi-Location

Built for growing businesses — supports multi-company, multi-branch, and multi-location operations out of the box with no extra setup.
