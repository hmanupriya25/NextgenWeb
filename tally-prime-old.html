    Everything TallyPrime does

    Features are built in and switched on as you need them, so a small business is not buried in options it will never use.

    Accounting and bankingInventory and manufacturingCosts, budgets and payrollReports and insightSecurity and controlConnected and mobile

    Accounting and banking

    Day-to-day books, billing and bank work in one place, with no add-ons to buy.

    • Complete accountingLedgers, vouchers, trial balance, profit and loss and balance sheet.
    • Invoicing and billing formatsPrint invoices in the layouts your customers expect.
    • Payments and receiptsTrack what is paid and received, bill by bill.
    • Credit managementKeep an eye on customer credit and overdue bills.
    • Cheques and e-paymentsPrint cheques and pay through e-payments with multiple banks.
    • Bank reconciliationMatch your books to the bank statement, with an improved reconciliation experience from Release 6.0.
    • Multi-currencyRecord transactions in foreign currencies.
    • Multiple companiesRun several businesses under the same licence.

    Inventory and manufacturing

    Know what you have, where it is and what it is worth.

    • Unlimited stock itemsGroup and classify items the way your business sells them.
    • Multiple godownsTrack stock by warehouse, outlet or location.
    • Batch and expiryFollow stock by batch and expiry date.
    • Stock valuation methodsChoose how stock is valued to suit your accounting policy.
    • Reorder levelsBe warned before an item runs out.
    • Bill of materialsDefine what goes into each finished product.
    • ManufacturingRecord production against your bill of materials.
    • Order processingRaise and follow sales and purchase orders.

    Costs, budgets and payroll

    See where money goes and what each team or project earns.

    • Cost centres and categoriesAllocate income and expenses to departments, projects or branches.
    • Budgets and controlsSet budgets for cost centres and track spending against them.
    • PayrollManage salaries and statutory payroll for your staff.
    • Profitability by unitCompare departments or projects on the same report.

    Reports and insight

    Answers on the screen in seconds, laid out the way you want them.

    • Go ToSearch from anywhere and jump to any of 400+ reports or screens.
    • MultitaskingSwitch between screens without losing the entry you were making.
    • Save ViewSave your own versions of a report with the columns and filters you use.
    • Personalised reportsAdjust any report to your business instead of exporting to Excel.
    • Real-time figuresReports update as vouchers are posted.
    • Reports in a browserOpen key reports on any device, including phones.

    Security and control

    Decide who can see and change what, and keep a record of it.

    • Role-based user rightsSet access by role and designation.
    • Feature-level securityLayer permissions down to specific features.
    • Password policySet your own rules for passwords.
    • Audit trailSee who changed what and when.
    • TallyVaultEncrypt company data so only you can open it.
    • Multi-user operationLet several people work at the same time.

    Connected and mobile

    Share, back up and check your business from outside the office.

    • WhatsApp sharingSend invoices, orders and reports straight from TallyPrime as PDF, JPEG or Excel.
    • Message templatesUse approved and personalised templates for reminders and statements.
    • TallyDrive backupAutomatic cloud backup: 1 GB free for single-user, 3 GB for multi-user.
    • Data synchronisationKeep data in step between two or more places of business.
    • Biz Analyst mobile appLive Tally data on Android and iPhone. See Biz Analyst.
    • Plug-ins and TDLExtend TallyPrime with plug-ins and custom TDL, built by NextGen.

    Silver or Gold

    The choice depends mainly on how many people use Tally. We will help you pick, and you can move up later.

    TallyPrime Silver

    For one person on one computer.

    • Users: single user
    • Runs on: a standalone PC
    • TallyDrive storage: 1 GB free

    TallyPrime Gold

    For teams who work at the same time.

    • Users: unlimited, multi-user
    • Runs on: several PCs on a LAN
    • TallyDrive storage: 3 GB free

    Common questions

    Does TallyPrime handle Sri Lankan tax?

    Our Sri Lanka Tax Module adds IRD schedules and VAT invoices to TallyPrime. Read about the module.

    Can several people use it at once?

    Yes, with the multi-user Gold edition. You set what each person can see and change.

    Is my data backed up?

    TallyDrive backs up your company data to the cloud automatically. We also set up local backups.

    Can I run it on the cloud?

    Yes. We host TallyPrime so your team can work from anywhere. See Tally on Cloud.

    Will you train my staff?

    Yes. Implementation includes setup, data import and training, and support or AMC keeps you covered afterwards.
